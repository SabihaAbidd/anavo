## Position and boundaries

Backend implementation references: [FastAPI response models](https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/tutorial/response-model/), [FastAPI testing](https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/tutorial/testing/), and [FastAPI CORS](https://fastapi.tiangolo.com/tutorial/cors/), official documentation checked 12 November 2026. These inform explicit Pydantic response contracts, actual TestClient/HTTPX endpoint tests, and an origin allowlist instead of wildcard CORS. Page publication dates were not specified. Pydantic 2.12.5 strict validation and cross-field behavior were directly exercised in the installed runtime; FastAPI itself remains uninstalled, so documentation review is not counted as actual API verification.

ANAVO's contribution is a Pakistan-focused, household-facing explanation and reproducible small-instance comparison, combining explicit mandatory requirements, a verifiable QUBO, an exact baseline and accessible UI. The research field already includes classical scheduling, gate-model quantum optimization, annealing, and quantum-inspired heuristics. These methods are distinct: a quantum-inspired classical optimizer is not proof of a quantum circuit execution, and annealing results do not validate QAOA.

Primary documentation and original/publisher research were preferred. Appliance ratings and priority values are illustrative user-editable examples. No source above supplies validated battery measurements or real household profiles for our presets. A general source about electricity reliability does not certify a particular household's outage conditions. Revisit the energy study full text and current NEPRA material before making more specific empirical claims.
